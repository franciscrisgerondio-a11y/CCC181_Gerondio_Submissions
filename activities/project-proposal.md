Gerondio | An app that lets students pre-order food from the school canteen. | Students and canteen vendors in MSU-IIT.| The canteen vendors should post on the app their menus for the day (or morning and afternoon) and the number of stocks. The number of stock would automatically go down after a student orders the item, and the items that are sold out should be marked as "sold out". Students can browse and choose an item, then order and pay through the app.  After choosing what to order, the students can choose a time frame/range for when they would pick up their orders, such as 10:00-10:10, 10:10-10:20. After choosing the time range, the payment can be done using e-wallets (GCASH, MAYA, etc.) or bank transfers(LANDBANK, MARIBANK, etc.) through a QR code that will show after finalizing the order. The order will only be finalized and acknowledged by the vendors after the app confirms the payment. Orders not claimed within 10 minutes after the allotted time slot should be flagged so that the vendors can resell them. There should be a reminder from the app when the chosen time is just 5 minutes away.|There are various time ranges when the school canteen in MSU-IIT gets very crowded, so moving inside alone is very difficult. Students who order inside waste so much of their time trying to move through the crowd, and the time wasted would be much longer because of payment (finding money from the wallet and waiting to receive the change). There were even some circumstances where students initially wanted to buy something inside, but after witnessing the horrific amount of people inside, they opted to no longer buy or go outside the campus to buy something.|

Gerondio | A mobile app that shows the user which bus is approaching, what type of bus it is, and how many seats are available so that the users can know if they can still ride on the bus. | Commuters in rural areas, especially students and workers who depend on buses to go to their respective schools and offices. Bus operators and conductors would also be part of the group since they will supply the data.| A GPS tracker should be installed in every bus so that the app can track where and when the bus will arrive in the users' area. Occupied seats would be known through a small screen installed at the back of all the bus seats, which will project the seat plan of the bus just like in the Philippine Airlines app, and then the commuter would mark their seat as taken, which will then be reflected in the app. There might be instances where commuters forget to mark their seats, so the conductor should double-check in a mini-master app if all commuters marked their seats properly. The app should remind the user if the bus is only 5 minutes away from the area. The app should be usable even if the internet is slow (but still needs internet).|Based on my experience in high school, rural commuters wait at the roadside around 2-5 hours to ride a bus. This happens because they do not know when the bus sets off, when the bus will arrive, and if the bus is full or not. Many buses pass without stopping because they are already full. This makes waiting very tiring and dangerous because in some areas there are no waiting sheds, and people would wait at the roadside, increasing the risk of a road accident.|
