## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
- I am designing this digital system for the Diyandi Community, composed of both the vendors and event attendees.

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
- I am doing this because I noticed that some vendors seem to always shout what they are selling without ever gaining customers, while other vendors seem to be crowded. Also, customers seem to roam the whole event area to find specific stalls without knowing where they are.

---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
- Vendors want to have their products and rates/prices known by the event attendees more effectively and efficiently. Customers want to find their perfect stall/shop without wasting time and energy roaming around the venue, unsure which direction they should go.

---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
- Vendors inside the venue have tarpaulins to show customers their products and rates; however, they don't show the whole list of their products. Because of this, customers always ask if they have this or if they have that, and many more questions; then they will leave because they can't find what they are specifically looking for, wasting time for both the customer and vendor.

---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?** 
- I would propose an application with a map of the whole venue. On the map, the various shops and stalls should be marked where they are specifically located. Once a customer clicks on the shop/stall, they can view all the products and the prices of the shop/stall. Vendors will have to list all of their products on the application; it should be very specific, with the prices included. Inside the application, there would be a navigation system to pinpoint the exact location of the shops/stalls, guiding users from their current location to the desired shop/stall. There would also be an intelligent search function to make it easier for customers to find their desired products. 

**How would it help the intended users?**  
- Vendors will have their products known to people beforehand, making it easier to attract customers. Customers can easily find the products, prices, and location of the shops and stalls.

---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. Vendors can put their products and prices/rates on the application to make disseminating their information easier.
2. Customers can search and filter for which shops/stalls have the specific products they want at specific prices.

---

## 6. Important qualities

Identify **two qualities** that would make your proposed system useful. You may consider whether it should be easy to use, fast, reliable, safe, private, accessible, multilingual, low-data, clear, or available during high demand.

### Quality 1: The application should be easy to use.

**Why does this matter to users?**  
- This quality matters because the goal of the application is to make it easier for people to find and locate their perfect shops and stalls. If using the application makes it so that it would have the reverse effect by making it hard to use, then it beats the objective of the application.

### Quality 2: The application provides an offline version of the most recent update encountered by the user (the version from the last time the user went online).

**Why does this matter to users?**  
- This matters to customers who have limited or no cellular internet. I noticed that inside the venue, cellular data is not very strong, so making the application have an offline function while maintaining its core functions should solve any problems that may arise when users start using it inside the venue.

---

## 7. How to tell whether the solution helped

How could you determine whether your proposed solution actually helped users?
- Ask shop/stall owners whether they have more customers or if attracting customers is easier after using the application.
- Ask customers whether navigating the venue was easier or harder after using the application.
